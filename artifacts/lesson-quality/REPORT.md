# FINLY — LESSON QUALITY PASS

Implementation covers the catalog, question edits, takeaway disclosures and validation. The requested **+30–40 seconds average duration is not established by measured learner timing**. No screens were added, and this report does not claim the entire duration objective is achieved.

1. **Lesson system audit.** Static TypeScript registry, Zod validation at import/build, 10 categories / 30 chapters. Supported production screens: situation, explanation, choices, true/false, scenario, calculation, case, remember and final. Legacy recap remains supported. Initial format enforced 16 screens / 9 questions / 5 minutes; structural validation now permits variable question counts. Lessons still end with remember and final.
2. **Total lessons audited:** 100 production lessons; the separate legacy quick lesson also passes schema/shuffle validation.
3. **Total questions audited:** 900, originally 9 per lesson. Final count remains 900.
4. **Questions kept:** 654 unchanged question definitions, apart from removing the universal case title where present. Existing good questions, explanations, cases, objectives, facts, sources and visible summaries are preserved by snapshot regression tests.
5. **Questions rewritten:** 246 selective interventions in 90 lessons. This includes 68 changed stems and 193 questions with option changes; these groups overlap. Many changes replace only implausible distractors, preserving the stem, correct answer and feedback.
6. **Questions added:** 0. Existing practice was retained and improved instead of adding slots that would also affect per-question XP and replay scores.
7. **Questions removed:** 0. IDs and the existing screen sequence remain stable.
8. **Examples replaced:** Dobânda q3 no longer asks only for 5% of 1.000 lei; it compares why a deposit result cannot determine the cost of a loan with a different period and commission. Rata lunară q5 now asks why 60 lei monthly does not establish the cheapest loan. Contract q2 applies the difference between the discussed and written program. Inflation's duplicate income question now compares a confirmed income rise with a larger rise in prices. Price and commission calculations remain when they inform a decision.
9. **Duration impact:** all integer metadata remains 5 minutes. Deeper choices, applied wording and optional explanations target more reflection. No claim of a measured 30–40-second average, no additional minute labels and no forced screen count. Timing with learners remains outstanding.
10. **Case-study prompts:** removed 279 content-driven universal titles. Questions without a title become their own heading. Optional `leadIn` belongs to content; Dobânda q6 has contextual information about needing money before maturity. No empty placeholder is rendered. The historical writer also no longer emits the universal phrase.

13. **Takeaway details rewritten:** 98 lesson-specific elaborations using the existing material. New details have two concise sentences; existing useful details in the previously authored lessons remain unchanged.
