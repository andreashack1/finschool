# Fini V2 character bible

Canonical identity: `fini-v2-reference.png`. The five user-supplied `ChatGPT Image Oct 17, 2026, …` expression PNGs are approved V2 expressions. See `public/brand/SOURCES.md` for exact provenance and mapping.

- Golden/tan fur; defined medium brown swept mane; blue eyes; cream muzzle; same nose, ears, expressive eyebrows and friendly facial proportions.
- Youthful, calm, confident, supportive, intelligent and slightly playful. Never preschool-like, angry or shaming.
- Blue casual hoodie with white drawstrings, kangaroo pocket and subtle white F. Navy trousers and white/blue sneakers in the canonical full-body image. No finance symbols, suits or props.
- Keep the same mane silhouette, torso/limb ratios, shoulders and golden tail with brown tuft. Preserve intentional hands, wrists, elbows and anatomy from approved sources; do not redraw or invent extra limbs.
- Home: softly smiling, hands together. Thinking: hand under chin. Completion: raised hand, open-mouth happy smile. Profile: stable warm direct-view portrait. Correct: closed-eye happy smile. Wrong: warm neutral. Serious: calm attentive neutral.

All production assets are transparent, lossless WebP exports under `public/brand/fini-v2/`. The canonical full-body cutout is `fini-v2-full-transparent.png`, derived with the image tool from the original reference without redesign. Existing expression alpha is preserved; no threshold background removal.

The `FinlyMascot` head/bust/full and six-mood API remains centralized. Head uses intentional portrait artwork, not a tiny full-body figure. For portrait-only expressions, full means the entire available upper-body pose; only neutral/wrong/serious have true full-body art. Missing art falls back to neutral V2. Future full-body expression variants can be added centrally. No avatar customization is implemented.
